ShelfSync Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the ShelfSync app ("ShelfSync", "the app") uses, how it is used, and what choices you have. ShelfSync is provided by Baker Chahin ("we", "us").

1. Information the app uses

With your permission (Clover's "Inventory – Read" permission), ShelfSync reads the following from your Clover inventory on your Clover device:

  • item names
  • item prices
  • product codes (UPC/EAN) and SKUs
  • item IDs assigned by Clover
  • category names, and which items are in each category (shown in the app as departments)

ShelfSync does not access customer information, payment or card data, orders, employee information, or any other data in your Clover account.

2. What the app stores

To detect price changes, ShelfSync keeps the following on your Clover device only:

  • a copy of each item's ID, name, code and the price at the time you saved a baseline or printed a label
  • the list of items in your most recent print batch, so it can be reprinted
  • a price change history: for each accepted price change, the item's ID, name, code, old and new price, and the date
  • your settings: label size, label design, the optional store name, and the optional store logo you choose, all printed on labels

3. How the information is used

The information is used only to:

  • show which items' prices have changed since your last baseline, for all items or one department
  • show the price change history, and create a spreadsheet (CSV) of it when you tap "Export spreadsheet"
  • print shelf labels (item name, price and barcode) on the printer connected to your Clover device

4. Sharing

ShelfSync does not send your information to us or to any third party. We do not operate servers that receive your data. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Labels are sent only to your own printer through Clover's printing service. An exported spreadsheet is saved on your Clover device, and goes elsewhere only if you choose "Share" and pick an app (such as email) to send it with. Your use of Clover devices and services is governed by Clover's own terms and privacy policy.

5. Keeping and deleting your information

Information stays on your Clover device until you remove it. You can clear saved prices at any time with "Clear saved baseline" in the app's menu. Price change history is deleted automatically after two years. Uninstalling ShelfSync deletes all information the app stored, except spreadsheets you exported, which stay where they were saved until you delete them.

6. Security

Information is stored on your Clover device and protected by the device's and Clover's security. Because we do not collect or transmit your data, it is not stored on our systems.

7. Children

ShelfSync is a business tool for merchants and is not directed to children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of ShelfSync after a change means you accept the updated policy.
