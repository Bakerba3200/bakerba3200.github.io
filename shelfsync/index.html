ShelfSync Support

ShelfSync is a Clover app that prints new shelf labels only for items whose price changed.

Getting started

  • Save your prices. Open ShelfSync and tap Save baseline. This records the current price of every item.
  • Change prices as usual in Clover Inventory.
  • Print what changed. In ShelfSync, tap Refresh, then Print changed. Only items whose price went up, went down, or are new get a label.

Common questions

No items show up. ShelfSync needs permission to read your inventory. Make sure you accepted its permissions when installing it, and that your items exist in Clover Inventory with prices.

Labels print on receipt paper. No label printer is connected, so ShelfSync used your receipt printer. Connect a label printer such as the Epson TM-L90 to your Clover Station or Mini.

Labels don't fit my stickers. Open the menu (⋮) → Label size and choose your label size, or enter a custom size in inches.

The printer ran out of labels in the middle of a batch. Load more labels, then open the menu → Reprint last batch.

I need just one label again. Tap the item in the list, then Print this label.

I want to start over. Open the menu → Clear saved baseline, then tap Save baseline again.
